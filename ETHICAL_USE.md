# Ethical use policy

FaultLoom is for defensive research on systems you own or are explicitly
authorized to test. Authorization must identify assets, identities, techniques,
time windows, budgets, external providers, data handling, and cleanup duties.
Ambiguity is a denial, not permission.

The following are outside v0.1 even if a configuration attempts to enable them:

- destructive actions or high-load testing;
- credential stuffing, password spraying, or account enumeration;
- unsolicited email, SMS, sales contact, account creation, or form submission;
- real payments, real-asset transfer, persistence, shells, or bulk data access;
- cloud metadata or internal assets absent from the signed authority grant;
- active testing of third parties without an applicable authorization; and
- automated vulnerability submission or customer-report delivery.

Use inert canaries and the minimum action needed to demonstrate an effect. Stop
after the minimum proof of another user's data and do not retain it. An operator
must review any scope draft and sign the resulting authority grant. A model,
scanner, browser, proxy, or plugin cannot expand that grant.
